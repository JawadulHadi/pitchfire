Guidelines

Usage Rules

✓ Do
  • Use the full-colour flame on dark backgrounds (Slate #0F172A or Night #060B18)
  • Keep "Pitch" and "Fire" as a single unbroken word in wordmark contexts
  • Pair Syne 800 for headlines with DM Sans for body copy
  • Maintain the flame's aspect ratio — never stretch or squish
  • Use the monochrome version on constrained surfaces (print, emboss)
  • Use Ember (#F97316) as the primary CTA color
✗ Don't
  • Place the full-colour logo on competing bright backgrounds without the dark badge
  • Separate "Pitch" from "Fire" with a space, dash, or line break
  • Recolour the flame gradients — the orange-to-gold gradient is the mark
  • Add drop shadows or outer strokes to the logo mark
  • Scale the icon below 24 × 24 px without switching to the simplified favicon variant
  • Use Violet (#7C3AED) as a primary action color — it is accent-only
